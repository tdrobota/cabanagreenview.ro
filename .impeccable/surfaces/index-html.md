# Surface brief: index.html (home, one page)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: Romanian groups of 8–16 planning a mountain weekend, mostly on phones.
Job: believe the cabin is real and right, then open the calendar and send a WhatsApp request.
Constraints: static, bilingual RO/EN, strict CSP, all facts as in PRODUCT.md; no invented price, rating or times.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The cabin seen at dusk through mountain haze, framed like a window. Refuses the category default of a cream "boutique lodge" page with serif display and stock-icon amenity cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep slate-teal (#0d1a1e → #22393f) and mist (#8fb0b7, #c9d8da), warm wood amber (#e9a23b) used only for the mark, stars and focus. Host Grotesk, light/regular, tight tracking; a two-tone heading where the follow-on clause drops to mist. Rounded inset frames (radius 44px, translucent 6px rim) over full-bleed photos; dark glass panels with darker inner fields; solid white buttons with near-black text.

STORY: First viewport: what (an A-frame), where (Rarău), for whom (8–16, min 2 nights) and the action. Then the cabin, the spec sheet of the house, photos, seasons, the map of what's nearby, guests' words, practical answers, and a close on the wordmark and contact.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed graded photo of the real A-frame (images/hero/cabin-mist). An inset rounded frame 24–48px from the edges holds the nav (mark + name left, links centre-right, white "Rezervă" button), a three-line headline top-left at ~8vw with the middle line in mist, the sub-paragraph bottom-left, a "9 km to Pietrele Doamnei" figure bottom-centre, and the glass booking card bottom-right (check-in / check-out fields, min stay, group size, white "Check availability" button opening the calendar).

FORM: Brief-pinned reference world (owner's Dribbble reference), code-led; no concept roll (pinned direction beats the roll). Seed key: none (pinned).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the hero card's date fields open the calendar and reflect the chosen dates back; the seasons stage re-uses the inset frame and cross-fades its photo per tab.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
